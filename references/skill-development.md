# Founder Skill Development

Use when building Section 7 of the Build Brief. Maps common founder gaps to fast development paths.

---

## Hard Skills by Business Type

### Software / SaaS
| Skill | Why It Matters | Fastest Path |
|---|---|---|
| Vibe coding / AI-assisted building | Ship an MVP without a developer | Cursor or Replit + Claude, build something in 48hrs |
| Landing page creation | Convert visitors before you have a product | Carrd, Framer — copy 3 great pages, then remix |
| Cold email copywriting | Get first customers without an audience | Study Alex Hormozi, Justin Welsh. Write 50 cold emails |
| Analytics setup (GA4, Mixpanel) | Know if your product is working | Take Google's free GA4 certification |
| SaaS pricing strategy | Most founders underprice | Read "Monetizing Innovation" or Lenny Rachitsky on pricing |

### Physical Products / E-commerce
| Skill | Why It Matters | Fastest Path |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier sourcing (Alibaba, local) | Find and vet manufacturers | Post on Alibaba, order samples from 3–5 suppliers |
| Product photography | Converts more than almost anything | Borrow a good camera, learn natural light, study top Shopify stores |
| Shopify store setup | Get to revenue fast | Shopify free trial + free YouTube walkthroughs |
| Facebook/Instagram ads | Scalable paid acquisition | Start with $10/day, run 5 ad variations, kill losers |
| Customs / import basics | Avoid getting inventory stuck | Flexport's free guides, or hire a customs broker for first shipment |

### Creator / Content Business
| Skill | Why It Matters | Fastest Path |
|---|---|---|
| Short-form video | Highest reach-per-hour of any channel right now | Post daily for 30 days, study top 10 creators in your niche |
| Email copywriting | Email converts 3–5x better than social | Morning Brew style: conversational, specific, value-first |
| Newsletter growth | Build an asset you own | Beehiiv or Substack, referral program, cross-promos |
| Editing (video/written) | Separates average from great content | Cut ruthlessly — remove anything that doesn't add |

### Services / Consulting
| Skill | Why It Matters | Fastest Path |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal writing | Closes more deals | Study 10 winning proposals, create a template |
| Scope management | Protects your time and margin | Write a clear SOW before every project |
| Pricing for value, not time | 10x your hourly rate | Read "Pricing Creativity" by Blair Enns |
| Case study creation | Your best marketing asset | Document one client result with before/after metrics |

## Soft Skills That Most Founders Underestimate

### Comfort with rejection
Why it matters: You'll hear "no" 20x before every "yes." Most people quit at 3.
Fastest path: Do 30 cold outreach messages in one week. The fear disappears after the first 10 non-responses.

### Public storytelling
Why it matters: Your story is your best marketing. If you can't tell it compellingly, you're invisible.
Fastest path: Record yourself pitching for 2 minutes. Watch it back. Cringe. Redo it 10 times. Post one.

### Asking for money
Why it matters: Most first-time founders give things away for free, then run out of runway.
Fastest path: Name a price in your next 5 conversations. Watch what happens.

### Hiring judgment
Why it matters: First 3 hires define culture and velocity. One wrong hire costs months.
Fastest path: Read "Who" by Geoff Smart. Do structured interviews with homework assignments.

### Investor communication
Why it matters: Even if you don't raise, investors are a signal for press, customers, and talent.
Fastest path: Write a weekly update email to 10 people who care about your progress. Get comfortable narrating your metrics.

## Timeline Calibration

Use this to set realistic expectations in the Build Brief:

| Skill | Time to "Good Enough" |
|---|---|
| Cold email / DM outreach | 1 week (just do it) |
| Landing page | 2–3 days |
| Short-form video | 2–4 weeks of daily posting |
| Paid ads | 4–6 weeks + $500 in test spend |
| Newsletter | 3–6 months to meaningful list |
| Sales conversations | 10–20 conversations |
| Fundraising pitch | 3 months of iteration with real feedback |
