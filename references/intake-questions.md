# Intake Questions — Extended Bank

Extended question banks by persona type. Load when the base SKILL.md questions aren't enough or when you've identified the user's persona.

---

## By Persona

### Student / Recent Graduate
- What's your major or field of study? What problem in that field annoys you most?
- What student communities, clubs, or forums are you active in?
- Do you have student discount access to tools, APIs, or cloud credits?
- What do other students pay for that you think is a bad deal?
- What have professors or classmates asked you to help them with?

### Working Professional (Employed)
- What do you do at work that nobody else on your team wants to do?
- What manual process at your job could be automated?
- What does your company pay for that you think is overpriced or could be replaced?
- What industry knowledge do you have that outsiders would pay to access?
- What do your colleagues complain about most?

### Freelancer / Consultant
- What's the most common request you get that you currently turn down?
- What part of your service do clients value most but you find easiest?
- What's a result you consistently deliver that clients struggle to find elsewhere?
- Could your service methodology be packaged into a product, course, or tool?

### Creator / Maker
- What platform are you on? How big is your audience?
- What do your followers ask you for most that you don't currently offer?
- What would your top 100 fans pay $10/month for?
- What do you make or create that people compliment you on?

### Immigrant / Expat / Third-Culture
- What products from your home country are unavailable or hard to find where you live now?
- What cultural practices, foods, or services from home do people in your current country not know about?
- What do you know about doing business in your home country that locals here don't understand?
- What's cheaper in your home country that people here overpay for?

### Serial / Repeat Founder
- What did you learn from your last venture that you'd apply immediately?
- What market do you understand better than almost anyone, because you've lived in it?
- What's the thing you *wish* existed that you keep telling other people to build?
- What relationships or distribution channels from your last venture are still warm?

### Hackathon / Speed Builder
- What's the theme or constraint of the hackathon?
- What can you ship in 24–48 hours with your current skills?
- What problem do you have personal experience with that you could prototype fast?
- Who's on your team? What are their strengths?

### Parent / Caregiver
- What problems do you face as a parent that you can't find a good solution for?
- What do other parents in your circle complain about or spend money on?
- What did you have to figure out the hard way that you could teach others?

## Deep-Dig Follow-Up Questions

Use these when a person gives a promising but vague answer:

**If they say "I'm good at design":**
- What type of design? For whom?
- Have you ever been paid for it?
- What's a project you're proud of?

**If they say "I want to make passive income":**
- What does passive mean to you — less than 5 hrs/week, or truly hands-off?
- Have you tried anything before?
- What's your timeline to first dollar?

**If they say "I have a big network":**
- What industry is this network in?
- How did you build it?
- Would they take a cold DM from you today?

**If they say "I want to build an app":**
- Can you code, or would you need to find someone who can?
- Why an app specifically — have you validated the problem without one?
- What app do you wish existed?

**If they say "I have a product idea already":**
- Jump to Phase 2 — treat this as the first candidate idea.
- Ask: Have you talked to anyone who would buy this? What did they say?
